About

Making machines easy to explain.

How a clock keeps time, how an engine turns, how a robot walks: these are some of the most satisfying things to understand, and some of the hardest to show. GearScript exists so that showing them takes a description, not days of animation.

What we believe

  • Describing should be enough. If you can say how the parts connect, you should be able to watch them move.
  • The motion should be right. Gears that mesh and linkages that close teach the truth; animations that only look right do not.
  • Your work belongs to you. A project is text you own, in a format anyone can read, with no account and nothing uploaded.
  • Made for people and for AI. A small language with clear errors helps a student, an engineer and a model alike.
  • Open by default. The language and the studio are free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.
