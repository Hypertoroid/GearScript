# Code of conduct

GearScript is built by people with very different backgrounds: engineers, teachers, students, animators, hobbyists. We want everyone to feel welcome to ask questions and share work.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone was new once.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Persistently disrupting discussions.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in any community space run by the project, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainers privately (see SECURITY.md for the contact). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who breaks these rules, in proportion to what happened.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
