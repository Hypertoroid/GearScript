Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what this Orrery site (“the studio”, “we”) collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. The site is run by . Questions or requests: .

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name or email.
  • Your projects stay in your browser. We do not upload the code you write or the files you open.
  • Analytics are off unless you allow them. If you do, we count which features are used, under a random id. No ads, no selling, no third-party trackers.
  • If this site offers the AI buttons (Generate, Fix with AI) and you use them, your request and your project's code are sent to Anthropic to write the reply. We do not keep the request or the reply.
  • Orrery is open source, so you can check all of this in the code.

What we collect

If you allow analytics

A random visitor id (in the ms_vid cookie) and a record of events such as opening the studio, choosing an example, copying the AI prompt, finishing the tour and exporting, with the time, the page and small details (for example which example, or the format and size of an export). These records are not linked to your name, email address or IP address.

If you use the AI features

Where this site offers them, when you press Generate or Fix with AI, the studio sends what you typed, the project's code and (if you tick it) a description of the scene at one moment to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API and returns the reply. We do not store the request or the reply. Do not put personal information in your request or your code. To limit how many AI requests one visitor can make in a day, the server keeps a count under a one-way hash of your IP address, mixed with a secret and with the date, so it changes every day; these counts are deleted after two days. Copy prompt sends nothing: it only copies text for you to paste wherever you choose.

Technical data

To protect the site from abuse, the server counts requests per visitor using the hash described above or the analytics id; your IP address itself is not written to our database. The company that hosts the site may keep standard request logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and operations; see Who we share data with.

Stored only in your browser

Your projects, 3D models you add, editor settings, the tour and your cookie choice are kept in your browser's storage. They never leave your device unless you save a project file yourself.

Loaded from other sites

The studio's 3D and video libraries come from this site. If they cannot be loaded (for example, a copy opened without its vendor folder), it fetches them from cdnjs (Cloudflare) or jsDelivr instead. Its fonts come from Google Fonts. If you tick Speak the lines into the video with a neural voice when exporting, the speech engine loads from jsDelivr and the voice files from Hugging Face. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when they send the files, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics events: 13 months, then deleted automatically.
  • AI requests and replies: not stored. Daily AI request counts: two days.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to deal with your request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, or Californian law), you can ask to see the data we hold about you, delete it, object to some uses, and withdraw consent. Because analytics records carry only a random id, tell us the id in your ms_vid cookie, or simply withdraw consent and clear your cookies. Write to ; we will reply within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

Children

The studio needs no account and asks for no personal details. Analytics run only with consent. If you believe a child's data has reached us without the consent the law requires, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We hold no accounts or passwords. Connections should be encrypted with HTTPS, requests from other sites are refused, and requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
